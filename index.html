THE PERSON BEHIND THE POST

A note from Lizelle

I have a career in tech. Alongside it, there has always been a quieter part of me that loves words, paper, warm drinks and small creative rituals.

When I discovered letter clubs, I found myself sketching, drafting letters and researching paper because I wanted to. Yours, From Here grew from that feeling.

I’m creating a little space to slow down, stay curious and enjoy your own company. You don’t need a new goal or a plan for what comes next. Just a moment to open a letter.

I’m glad you’re here.

A FEW PRACTICAL THINGS

Before the
first letter.

Something else you’re wondering?
More joining details will be available at launch.

What comes inside each envelope?

A personal letter, an art postcard, a small coordinating sticker sheet, and a reading-and-curiosity foldout with a book recommendation, a sourced fascination and an optional activity or solo-date idea. A handmade extra may appear from time to time.

Do I need to join at a particular time of year?

No. The editions are numbered, rather than tied to calendar months. Your first envelope is Begin Here, followed by the next edition each month.

Is this a course or a book subscription?

It’s a personal letter club. There are no lessons to complete or progress targets. You’ll receive book recommendations, but the books themselves are not included.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. The club will be a monthly subscription with no minimum commitment. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals. The launch terms will explain how already-paid envelopes are handled.

Where will you post, and when?

Shipping destinations, postage costs and dispatch dates are still being finalised. These will be clearly available before you can place an order.

Are subscriptions open yet?

Not yet. This is the first website preview. Membership pricing, dispatch details and checkout will be added before the club opens.
